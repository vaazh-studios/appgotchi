Appgotchi Pulse · 2.1 prototype

Pulse

Ratings, new reviews and crash rate for every app, at desktop and phone width.

1. Today 2. App detail 3. Link apps

1. Today

One row per app, with ratings and crash rate in a right-aligned column. Linked iOS and Android apps share a row. Apple doesn’t report a crash rate, so Tidepool shows none. Select any app to open the Sproutly detail below.

Downloads and proceeds stay locked per app until that app’s vendor number or reports folder is added.

2. App detail

Sproutly, linked across both stores. Hover the crash chart, or focus it and use the arrow keys. The review filter works. Reviews are read-only.

3. Link apps

Opened from Change on the app detail screen. Try Change, Unlink and the Link with menus. Both frames share the same state.
